# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Notilify contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to help keep participation open, respectful, and free from harassment.

## Expected behaviour

- communicate with empathy and respect
- welcome constructive feedback and different levels of experience
- focus technical disagreements on the work, not the person
- respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information
- stop behaviour that a maintainer identifies as disruptive or unsafe

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualised attention, sustained disruption, and deliberate disclosure of private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to `support@notilify.com`. Maintainers will review reports confidentially and take proportionate action, including content removal or temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

This policy applies in repository discussions and when someone is publicly representing the Notilify project.
